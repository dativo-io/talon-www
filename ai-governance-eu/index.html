Controls

What Talon adds to AI traffic.

Talon sits at the provider boundary. It checks policy before requests are forwarded and records signed evidence after each decision.

🔐

PII and scanner controls

Detect, redact, warn, or block sensitive data with built-in regex, Presidio-compatible services, or a local LLM scanner such as Ollama. Scanner failures stay fail-closed in enforcement paths.

🇪🇺

Provider, region, and fallback control

Use eu_strict, eu_preferred, or global routing rules. On transient failures, ordered fallback candidates re-run sovereignty, provider/model, tool, budget, and session policy before dispatch.

🧾

Signed evidence and reports

Record policy decisions, PII findings, scanner identity, failover attempts, model, cost, destination, signature, and sovereignty posture.

Policy-safe reliability

Provider fallback without turning an outage into a policy bypass.

Talon v1.6.7 walks an ordered fallback chain only for transient upstream failures such as timeouts, connection errors, HTTP 429, and 5xx responses. Every candidate is re-evaluated against the full caller policy surface. Under eu_strict, a non-EU/LOCAL candidate is not dispatched, even when it could restore availability.

Air-gap / local-only

For teams that need stronger than “prefer EU”.

Air-gap mode is a stricter deployment posture: it implies eu_strict, requires explicit crypto keys, applies deny-by-default EU/LOCAL egress when no custom egress policy is set, and guards upstream HTTP against surprise destinations. Pair it with a local Ollama scanner when detection must also stay on your own hardware.

Air-gap guideLocal scanner enginesPosture reportProvider registry
sovereignty:
  mode: eu_strict
  deployment_mode: air_gap

scanner:
  type: llm
  llm:
    base_url: http://127.0.0.1:11434

# LLM provider and PII scanning can stay LOCAL
