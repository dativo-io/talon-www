Controls

What Talon adds to AI traffic.

Talon sits at the provider boundary. It checks policy before requests are forwarded and records signed evidence after each decision.

🔐

PII and content controls

Detect, redact, warn, or block sensitive data before it reaches an LLM provider.

🇪🇺

Provider and region control

Use eu_strict, eu_preferred, or global routing rules. Air-gap mode forces eu_strict and local/EU egress discipline.

🧾

Signed evidence and reports

Record policy decisions, PII findings, model, cost, destination, signature, and sovereignty posture.

Air-gap / local-only

For teams that need stronger than “prefer EU”.

Air-gap mode is a stricter deployment posture: it implies eu_strict, requires explicit crypto keys, applies deny-by-default EU/LOCAL egress when no custom egress policy is set, and guards upstream HTTP against surprise destinations. It is useful for local Ollama tests, private EU model endpoints, or regulated customer reviews.
