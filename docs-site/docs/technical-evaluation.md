# Evaluate Talon

Determine whether Talon fits your applications and operating requirements. Evaluate the selected release and the controls that matter to your organization.

## Understand the architecture

[Control plane overview](./control-plane.md) · [Choose your integration path](./choosing-integration-path.md) · [Trust and enforcement boundaries](./control-plane-boundaries.md) · [Governance control matrix](./governance-control-matrix.md).

## Verify a control yourself

Try the [no-key demo](./quickstart-demo.md), then [connect an existing application](./add-talon-to-existing-app.md). For business actions, reproduce the [exact-action proof](./action-gateway-proof.md). Verify [signed evidence](./evidence-store.md) and record allowed and denied behavior using the [evaluation checklist](./evaluation-checklist.md).

## Review security and compatibility

Read the [threat model](./threat-model.md), [authentication and key scopes](./authentication-and-key-scopes.md), [provider registry](./provider-registry.md), [conformance](./conformance.md), [benchmarks](./benchmarks.md) and [release limitations](https://github.com/dativo-io/talon/blob/v1.17.0/LIMITATIONS.md). Historical examples do not guarantee current client compatibility.

## Assess operational readiness

Review the [Operations Console](./operations-console.md), [single-node production profile](./single-node-production.md), [incident response](./incident-response-playbook.md) and [production pilot runbook](./production-pilot.md). Community's supported production profile is single-node. Verify recovery and rollback, not only successful requests.

## Choose your adoption path

Compare [Talon Community and Enterprise](./open-source-and-enterprise.md). Enterprise integrations, support and scale profiles are scoped and validated during evaluation.

[Discuss a pilot](https://dativo.io/pilot/) to explore how to scale and operate AI across your organization.

Talon does not govern bypassed paths, guarantee provider invoice ceilings or establish regulatory compliance. Approval authorizes an exact operation; it does not itself dispatch it.
