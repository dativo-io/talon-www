Website analytics and privacy

This notice describes the public website, not the data processed by a self-hosted Talon installation.

Website delivery

The site is delivered through Cloudflare. Delivery and security services may process technical request information to serve pages and protect the service.

Website analytics

We use Umami Cloud to understand page visits and interest in product information, documentation and pilot evaluation. We configure the tracker to exclude URL query strings and fragments and respect browser Do Not Track. Our custom events send page paths, predefined interaction labels and allowlisted campaign values; they do not intentionally send link text, copied commands, calendar link tokens or contact details.

Analytics may include browser, device, referrer, approximate location and session information as described by Umami. See Umami's privacy information and Cloudflare's privacy information.

External services

Pilot scheduling opens Google Calendar, while open-source evaluation links to GitHub. Those services have their own privacy practices. The website does not contain a contact or workflow-upload form. Do not include personal details in campaign URLs.

Questions and security reports

For questions about website privacy or security, contact Dativo privately at security@dativo.io. Do not post sensitive details in public GitHub issues. The project security policy describes vulnerability reporting for Talon.
