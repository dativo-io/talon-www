The pilot contract

One use case. One failure. One control. One receipt.

  1. Route one development or internal use case through Talon.
  2. Observe in shadow mode without changing the response.
  3. Trigger one cost, reliability or policy condition.
  4. Inspect the session and verify the signed evidence.

Good pilot candidates

Start where the operating pain is already visible.

The best pilot is not “govern all AI.” It is a bounded scenario your team can reproduce and judge.

Unexpected or fragmented spendProve that a new request is stopped before provider access when the effective budget is crossed.
Provider failure behaviorTrigger a supported transient failure and confirm fallback stays inside the same policy boundary.
Customer or confidential dataSee what Talon would redact, block or keep inside an allowed destination before enabling enforcement.
Unclear agent sessionsReconstruct cost, provider path, denials and decisions for one asserted session.

Talon governs traffic and actions routed through its interception paths. It does not orchestrate agents or observe local actions that bypass it.
