The scope

One workflow, one verifiable result

01 / Your workflow

Keep what already works.

Bring a current support bot, coding assistant, n8n automation or application. Identify the model call or exact action you can route through Talon.

02 / Your boundary

Define one concrete decision.

Examples: deny a disallowed provider, enforce a spending limit, require approval for an exact refund action, or record a defensible policy decision.

03 / Your proof

Test the decision and evidence.

Reproduce an allow, deny or supported approval case in a bounded evaluation. Inspect the recorded decision and signed evidence, including what Talon cannot observe.

How to prepare for your first use case

No installation or code changes are needed before the call. Just bring three things:

  • Your workflow: pick one existing AI use case, such as customer support, document processing or an n8n workflow.
  • Your setup: tell us which AI providers, applications and orchestration tools you use. A high-level description is enough.
  • Your goal: choose one result to test, such as a spending limit, sensitive data protection or approval before a business action.

We’ll identify a supported integration path and agree on a small pilot if it fits. Please do not share credentials or production customer data.

Talon governs only model traffic and actions routed through supported boundaries. Human approval authorizes an exact operation; it does not itself execute a business action. Evidence is verifiable, not proof of unobserved activity or automatic compliance.
