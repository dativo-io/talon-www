Each new app should not invent another budget implementation, provider-failure path, data policy, tool boundary and incident trail. Talon keeps the application and orchestrator in place while applying one company operating baseline to every use case routed through it.

Fallback remains inside the operating contract.

On supported transient failures, Talon evaluates an explicitly configured fallback chain. Every candidate is re-checked against model, provider, sovereignty and budget constraints; exhausted chains fail closed.
