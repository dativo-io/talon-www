How it works

n8n orchestrates. Talon authorizes and dispatches.

01

Register the AI use case

Run talon agents add, receive its key, connect the importable workflow and configure the exact action under company and use-case policy.

02

Decide or request approval

Talon binds the operation and typed arguments. Reviewers have their own credentials and can approve or reject requests in the CLI or reviewer API.

03

Claim and verify

n8n claims the authorized operation. Talon dispatches to the business adapter and records decision, review, attempt and outcome in verifiable evidence.
