Document workflows with spending limits

The existing n8n quarterly-document workflow demonstrates a different failure mode: as an agent-scoped session approaches its estimate-based budget cap, Talon prevents the next model request and the application preserves already completed summaries. This is a supporting cost-control proof, not an independent top-level product or a guarantee against one-request overspend.

What they share

One policy foundation; use-case-specific effective controls.

Authenticate the use case, resolve company policy and its constrained overrides, enforce only at supported boundaries, and inspect request/session/operation evidence where available. Talon does not own the business workflow, execute the external agent loop, or observe direct calls that bypass interception.
