Planned Enterprise integrations

Fit the systems your company already operates.

01 · Identity

Corporate SSO and domain

OIDC with verified organization/issuer binding, mapped groups, separate operator and reviewer rights, and deprovisioning. An email suffix alone never confers access.

02 · Security

SIEM and SOC workflows

Route correlated enforcement and administrative events into one qualified customer SIEM. OSS retains canonical signed evidence, generic telemetry and independent verification.

03 · Human decisions

Existing approval systems

Connect a chosen Teams, Slack or ITSM workflow to canonical exact-action approvals. A message is not a credential and approving does not dispatch an action.
