Community & Enterprise

One control plane.
Built for every AI use case.

Start with open-source Talon Community to enforce company policy on the AI calls and actions you connect. Talon Enterprise extends the same foundation for teams that need corporate identity, controlled changes and company-wide operations.

Try a pilot ↗ Explore Community ↗

Talon Community is open source under Apache 2.0.

What Enterprise adds

From one operator to many teams.

The policy and evidence engine doesn't change. Enterprise brings that foundation into the people, processes and security systems your organization already uses.

01

Teams connect their own AI apps

Support can register its assistant and issue its runtime credential without waiting for the platform team. Company-wide policy still cannot be weakened.

02

Know who changed what

Track who updated a policy, issued a key or changed an access right, when it happened, and whether it succeeded. Separate from AI decision evidence.

03

Fit your company systems

Connect corporate sign-in, reviewer workflows, security monitoring and key custody—starting with the integration your pilot actually needs.
